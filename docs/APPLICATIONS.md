[Русский](APPLICATIONS.ru.md) | **English**

## The applicability test

Dispatch Core is a candidate when most answers are “yes”:

1. Does a request, route, inspection or internal task need to be dispatched?
2. Does a person or crew perform it away from the dispatcher?
3. Do status, position or ETA reduce calls and uncertainty?
4. Is completion proved by photo, comment, signature, code, measurement or
   checklist?
5. Is today's coordination happening in calls, chats and spreadsheets?

Not every niche below is a separate product. They are discovery prompts for a
buyer and candidates for declarative industry packs.

## Product conclusion

The universal asset is not a giant dynamic form builder. It is a small set of
stable primitives:

- `WorkOrder`, `WorkType`, `Actor`, `Crew`, `Asset`, `Place`, `Route`;
- assignment or competitive acceptance;
- statuses and deadlines;
- tracking session and checkpoints;
- configurable evidence and report;
- event/outbox/audit;
- generator, worker transport, storage and egress ports.

An industry pack names these primitives, chooses fields and evidence, and adds
workflows only where the generic lifecycle genuinely cannot express the job.
That gives broad applicability without pretending that one operational core is
already a certified CRM for every industry.

The catalog is a synthesis, not a claim that one competitor supports every
line. It was cross-checked against current public product catalogs:

  refrigeration, alarm, AV, dock/door and fire/life-safety work:
  <https://www.servicetitan.com/industries>.

  transportation, telecom, waste, retail and home health:
  <https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/field_service_basics/field_service_basics_intro>.
- Delivery products apply dispatch, live status and proof of delivery to courier,
  grocery, restaurant, pharmacy, furniture and construction flows:
